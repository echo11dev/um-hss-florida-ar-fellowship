Department of Orthopaedics

SF Match

Positions are filled through the SF Match. Applications go through the SF Match central application service. Deadlines are the dates posted there. The program conducts interviews between November and January.

Open SF Match

Eligibility

  • Successful completion of an ACGME-accredited residency, an ACGME International Advanced Specialty program, an AOA-approved program, or a program accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada or the College of Family Physicians of Canada.
  • USMLE Steps 1, 2, and 3 completed before the fellowship starts.
  • One position. The fellowship is one year. No call is required.

Visas

For applicants who are not U.S. citizens or permanent residents, Jackson Health System states that the J-1 Alien Physician visa, sponsored by ECFMG, is the standard visa. Questions about eligibility go to the Jackson physician staffing office listed on the program page.
