Department of Orthopaedics

The division has a high surgical volume and operates five days a week. The fellow’s week is four days in the operating room and one day in clinic. There is no call requirement.

What the year includes

The entire year is adult reconstruction of the hip and knee. The published case mix is primary and revision surgery, including arthritis, avascular necrosis, dysplasia, infection, and periprosthetic fracture. The program director’s charge to the fellow is mastery of primary arthroplasty, revision arthroplasty, periprosthetic joint infection, and periprosthetic fracture.

  • Primary total hip
  • Primary total knee
  • Revision hip
  • Revision knee
  • Arthritis
  • Avascular necrosis
  • Dysplasia
  • Periprosthetic joint infection
  • Periprosthetic fracture
Annual case counts live in the fellow’s ACGME case log. After AAHKS recognition in 2024, the program also shares case-log information with AAHKS. The Miller School and Jackson pages describe the volume as high and describe the weekly schedule above. They do not publish a single yearly total, so this page does not estimate one.

Where the cases are done

Published fellowship rotations are at two hospitals on the medical campus:

  • University of Miami Hospital, 1400 NW 12th Avenue, Miami, FL 33136.
  • Jackson Memorial Hospital, 1611 NW 12th Avenue, Miami, FL 33136.

Residents join the service as a PGY-2, PGY-4, and PGY-5. The fellow teaches in clinic and in the operating room and can mentor medical students on the service.
