Department of Orthopaedics

These are the faculty named on the Miller School Adult Reconstruction Fellowship page. Titles follow that page. Full biographies and photographs are on the faculty profiles.

MD

Michele R. D’Apuzzo, M.D.

Professor of Orthopaedic Surgery. Director of the Adult Reconstruction and Joint Replacement Fellowship.

Dr. D’Apuzzo completed his adult reconstruction fellowship at Hospital for Special Surgery. He trained in orthopaedic surgery at Mayo Clinic and the University of Virginia and is certified by the American Board of Orthopaedic Surgery. He is a Fellow of the American Orthopaedic Association. The Hip Society selected him for the Otto Aufranc Award in 2026. The Knee Society selected him for the John Insall Award in 2014.

View full profile · Clinical profile

VH

Victor Hugo Hernandez, M.D., M.S.

Professor of Orthopaedic Surgery. Chief, Division of Arthroplasty and Adult Joint Reconstruction.

Dr. Hernandez graduated from Universidad del Norte in Colombia. He completed orthopaedic residency at the University of Puerto Rico, a master’s degree in clinical and translational research there, and a fellowship in joint replacement and adult reconstruction at the Rothman Institute. His practice includes primary and complex revision hip and knee reconstruction.

View full profile · Clinical profile

CM

Colin McNamara, M.D., M.B.A.

Assistant Professor of Orthopaedic Surgery, Joint Replacement.

Dr. McNamara is a fellowship-trained adult reconstruction surgeon with a particular interest in complex revision hip and knee replacement, including infection and other modes of failure. He also performs primary hip and knee replacement for arthritis, avascular necrosis, and post-traumatic cartilage damage. He completed orthopaedic residency at University of Miami/Jackson Memorial and adult reconstruction fellowship at Houston Methodist. He earned his M.D. and M.B.A. at Florida Atlantic University.

Fernando E. Vilella-Hernandez, M.D.

Assistant Professor of Orthopaedic Surgery. Listed on the fellowship faculty. Jackson Health System also lists him as Chief of the Division of Orthopaedic Trauma.

Dr. Vilella-Hernandez is named on both the Miller School fellowship page and the Jackson Health System fellowship page. The department profile is the place for his training and clinical scope.
