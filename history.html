Fellowship / History

History of the fellowship

Department of Orthopaedics

The accredited program is the University of Miami/Jackson Health System Adult Reconstruction Fellowship in the Department of Orthopaedics. This site uses the name University of Miami / Hospital for Special Surgery Florida because the department’s musculoskeletal program at UHealth SoLé Mia is the clinical home of the UHealth–HSS collaboration announced in 2025.

  1. 2020

    The fellowship opens

    The Department of Orthopaedics launched the joint reconstruction fellowship in 2020. In May of that year the division posted a one-year position, with a start that August, and rotations at University of Miami Hospital and Jackson Memorial Hospital. On June 4, 2020, SF Match confirmed program ID 5706 for the Jackson Memorial Hospital / University of Miami Adult Reconstruction Fellowship. By December 2022 the department was training its third fellow.

  2. 2022

    ACGME accreditation

    On December 5, 2022, the Miller School announced that the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education had accredited the University of Miami/Jackson Health System Adult Reconstruction Fellowship. The announcement said accreditation was then held by about 22 percent of U.S. orthopaedic fellowships, and it described the Miller School program as the only ACGME-accredited orthopaedic adult reconstruction fellowship in Florida at that time. The department was training its third fellow. Accreditation also preceded a $20,000 educational grant from the American Association of Hip and Knee Surgeons. The ACGME program code is 2611126040.

  3. 2024

    AAHKS recognition

    On June 25, 2024, the AAHKS Fellowship Committee and Board of Directors approved the program’s application for AAHKS Fellowship Recognition. The program remains on the AAHKS-recognized list published January 12, 2026, under the name University of Miami Adult Reconstruction Fellowship. Fellows log cases in the ACGME case log system. The program also reports case-log information to AAHKS.

  4. 2025

    UHealth and HSS Florida

    On January 14, 2025, UHealth and Hospital for Special Surgery announced a collaboration to provide musculoskeletal care at UHealth SoLé Mia in North Miami. The department chair, Francis J. Hornicek, Jr., M.D., Ph.D., described the collaboration as pairing HSS with the Miller School faculty. Lee D. Kaplan, M.D., is medical director of the integrated UM/HSS orthopedics musculoskeletal program. Surgical services at SoLé Mia opened in October 2025, which InventUM described as the launch of the UHealth–HSS collaboration. HSS Florida’s own hospitals are in Palm Beach County. The Miami academic partnership is the SoLé Mia program.

  5. 2026

    The fellowship today

    The program still offers one ACGME-accredited position each year through SF Match. Michele R. D’Apuzzo, M.D., is the program director. Victor Hugo Hernandez, M.D., M.S., is chief of the Division of Arthroplasty and Adult Joint Reconstruction. The Miller School fellowship page lists the current teaching faculty.
