Department of Orthopaedics

The program offers one clinical fellow each year. The Department of Orthopaedics launched the fellowship in 2020, and by December 2022 it was training its third fellow. Names of graduates are not published on the Miller School or Jackson Health System pages. Those cells stay blank until the program office confirms each public listing.
