# University of Miami / HSS Florida Adult Reconstruction Fellowship

Static site for the Adult Reconstruction Fellowship in the Department of Orthopaedics at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine. The layout follows the department fellowship pages: mission, program director’s welcome, fast facts, clinical experience, and faculty, plus history, fellows, and case volume.

The accredited program remains the University of Miami/Jackson Health System Adult Reconstruction Fellowship (ACGME 2611126040). Official copy lives on the [Miller School page](https://med.miami.edu/departments/ortho/education/fellowships/orthopedic-surgery-adult-reconstruction-fellowship) and the [Jackson Health System page](https://graduate.jacksonhealth.org/program/orthopedic-surgery-adult-reconstruction-fellowship/).

## Edit

- Fellow names, residencies, and practices: `fellows.html`
- Case-log totals, when the program office publishes them: `volume.html`
- Faculty: `faculty.html`

Do not invent alumni names or yearly case counts. Leave a cell blank until the program office confirms it.
